HOW TO PLAY

Goal — race all 4 tokens around the board and into your home triangle. First to bring all four home wins.

Leaving base — you need a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square.

Extra turns — rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or bringing a token home earns another roll.

Three sixes — roll three 6s in a row and your turn is forfeited. (In two-dice mode: three consecutive doubles.)

Captures — land on an opponent's token to send it back to base… unless it stands on a ★ safe square, where no one can be captured.

Blockades — two or more of your tokens on one square form a blockade: opponents can neither pass nor capture it.

Exact finish — you need the exact roll to enter home. Overshoot and that token can't move.

Two dice — split the two values across tokens however you like; a 6 on either die can bring a token out; doubles roll again.
